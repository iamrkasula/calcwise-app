Support

Need help? Check the answers below, or write to us on the feedback board. Please include your device model and iOS version.

Results

Why is my bank's EMI slightly different?

Banks may count interest daily, start it from the day the money is paid out, round differently or add fees. Calcwise uses the standard monthly formula, so expect small differences.

Does Calcwise keep my calculations?

No. Calcwise doesn't save calculations. What you last typed in each calculator stays on your device so it's there when you come back. Share a result as a PDF, image or text to keep it.

Does Calcwise work without internet?

Yes. Every calculator works in Airplane Mode.

Country schemes

Which countries have schemes?

India, the United States, the United Kingdom, Canada and Australia. Pick yours in Settings › Country schemes. Calcwise has no tax calculators.

Why does a scheme say "as of"?

Official rates and limits change. Each one shows when it applies from, and you can change it to your own value. They are checked against official sources before each release.

Currency

How do I change the currency?

Settings › Currency. Amounts use that currency's symbol and digit grouping, for example lakh and crore for rupees.
