Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

Calcwise is a financial calculator. Every calculation happens on your iPhone or iPad. The amounts, rates and names you type never leave your device. Calcwise has no account and shows no ads.

Anonymous usage counts

Calcwise counts how the app is used with TelemetryDeck (TelemetryDeck GmbH, Germany), so we can see which calculators people use and what to improve. A count says things like "a loan calculator was opened", "a schedule was viewed" or "the currency was changed to INR", together with the app version, device model, iOS version, language, region and text-size setting.

A count never contains an amount, rate, result, name, search text or anything you typed. Each device is identified only by a random value that TelemetryDeck hashes twice, so it can't be traced back to you. Counts are not linked to you, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone else.

Crash reports

If the App Store or TestFlight version of Calcwise crashes or stops responding, it sends a crash report to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), so the problem can be found and fixed. A report contains the technical details of the crash: the app version, the code that was running, the device model, iOS version, free memory and similar device state, and a random install identifier.

A report does not contain your name, email, the amounts or names you typed, screenshots or a recording of the screen. IP addresses are not stored. Reports are not linked to you, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone else.

Calcwise Pro purchases

Calcwise Pro is an optional one-time in-app purchase. Apple handles the payment; Calcwise never sees your card, Apple Account or name. To check whether Pro is unlocked, restore it on another device, and test which paywall works best, the app uses Adapty (Adapty Tech Inc.). Adapty receives the purchase details from Apple (which product, its price, currency and date, and the store country), a random profile identifier that Adapty creates for this install, and the app version, device model, iOS version, language and time zone.

Adapty does not receive your name, email, Apple Account, IP address, advertising identifier or anything you typed. The purchase details are not linked to you, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone else.

Third-party services

Calcwise uses three services, each only for the purpose below:

  • TelemetryDeck (TelemetryDeck GmbH, Germany) receives the anonymous usage counts described above, to show which calculators are used. Privacy policy · Terms
  • Adapty (Adapty Tech Inc., USA) receives the purchase details described above, to unlock Calcwise Pro and compare paywall versions. IP address and advertising-identifier collection are turned off. Privacy policy · Terms
  • Sentry (Functional Software, Inc., USA) receives the crash and hang reports described above, to find and fix problems. IP address storage is turned off and its data scrubbing is on. Privacy policy · Terms

All three services are bound by their own policies and terms to protect this data at least as well as this policy does. None of them receives anything that identifies you.

What is stored on your device

  • What you last typed in each calculator, so it is there when you come back.
  • Your favourites and settings (currency, country schemes, decimals, theme).

This stays on the device and is not synced to iCloud. Deleting the app deletes it.

What Calcwise does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.
  • No saving or uploading of calculations.

Keeping and deleting data

  • What is stored on your device stays there and is deleted when you delete the app.
  • Usage counts, crash reports and purchase details are kept only as long as TelemetryDeck's, Sentry's and Adapty's retention periods (see their policies), then deleted. Apple keeps its own record of your purchase so you can restore it.
  • Deleting the app stops all counts and reports.
  • Because nothing is linked to you, we can't look up data from your device; if you have a question or a deletion request, contact us below with the date and device model and we will help.

Children

Calcwise is a general-audience calculator. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
